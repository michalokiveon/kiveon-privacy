Privacy Policy for Kiveon Games

Last updated: 17 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games and apps published by Kiveon, including Jelly Climb, World of Treasures, and any other games or apps released by Kiveon on Google Play or other app stores.

In this Privacy Policy, “Kiveon,” “we,” “our,” or “us” refers to the developer and publisher of these games and apps. “Game” or “App” means Jelly Climb, World of Treasures, and any other game or application published by Kiveon.

Information We Collect

Our games are designed as casual entertainment games. We do not require users to create an account to play Jelly Climb.

At this time, our games do not directly collect, store, or share personal information from users.

Our games may use standard Android, Google Play, and app store services required for installation, app delivery, updates, purchases if enabled, crash handling, and normal operation. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

If we receive limited technical or support-related information, we may use it only to:

  • operate and improve our games;
  • fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues;
  • improve gameplay, balance, and stability;
  • respond to user support requests;
  • comply with legal, platform, or app store requirements.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell user data.

We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Limited technical information may be processed by platform providers such as Google Play when needed for installation, updates, app delivery, purchases if enabled, and normal platform operation.

Children

Our games are intended to be family-friendly casual games. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation and delete the information where required.

Our games may use third-party services in the future, such as app store services, crash reporting, analytics, ads, leaderboards, cloud saves, or payment services.

If such services are added, this Privacy Policy and the relevant app store declarations will be updated to reflect the app’s data practices.
